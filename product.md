# Rawabet Trade (روابط للتجارة / Bonds Trade) Product Brief

## Product

Rawabet Trade (شركة روابط للتجارة) presents an Arabic-language investment opportunity focused on
financing trade transactions in the industrial and chemicals sector. The page explains
the Sharia-compliant Murabaha model, details the profit-distribution policy (3% quarterly cash advances with annual 50% net profit settlement), displays the exit strategy (anytime exit with full capital and retained earnings returned within 90 days), presents the actual quarterly track record (normalized to a 1-year equivalent), provides a conservative return simulator modeling a 30% annual return (15% cash dividends and 15% retained growth), and invites visitors to contact the executive team directly.

## Intended audience

Arabic-speaking prospective investors seeking Sharia-compliant, tangible asset-backed investment opportunities who want to evaluate the quarterly cash payouts and annual returns before contacting the team.

## Value proposition

The page describes a Sharia-compliant Murabaha trade-financing opportunity with short 30–90 day transaction cycles, 3% quarterly cash advances, year-end profit settlement, anytime exit within 90 days, and capital growth.

## Main user needs

1. Understand what the investment funds and how the Murabaha trading cycle works.
2. Calculate expected quarterly cash distributions and conservative annual returns.
3. Review actual historical performance (normalized on a 1-year basis).
4. Review liquidity and exit rules (anytime exit with capital and retained earnings returned within 90 days).
5. Contact the executive leadership (Alsayed Tawakol and Sameh El Gohary) directly.

## Current page capabilities
 
 - Arabic RTL investment presentation for شركة روابط للتجارة (Bonds Trade).
 - Clear narrative separating the Murabaha business opportunity from the audited track record.
 - Distribution policy: 3% quarterly cash advance on capital, with an annual financial year-end settlement allocating 50% of net realized profit in cash (net of advances) and retaining 50% for equity compounding.
 - Liquidity & Exit Strategy: Investors can submit an exit request at any time and receive their full principal capital plus retained earnings within 90 days.
 - Actual performance track record presented via a Mobile-First Annual Performance Stack (year cards with quarter tiles, zero horizontal scrolling) and a 100k Realized Wealth Proof card, showing opening 10.00 EGP to current 12.36 EGP, +23.6% capital growth, 2.36 EGP cash payout (+23,600 EGP cash per 100k), 47.2% total return over 15 months, normalized to 37.8% annualized.
 - 100% payout execution consistency proof (5 consecutive quarters paid on time) and audited report request action.
 - 4-pillar risk governance and physical asset-backing disclosure.
 - Direct contact links for Alsayed Tawakol (Managing Director) and Sameh El Gohary (CFO), with pre-filled WhatsApp messaging and a callback request form.

## Content and trust requirements

- Clearly distinguish historical/actual values from forecasts and scenarios.
- Label every figure with its currency, period, and whether it is actual or
  projected.
- Keep risk and non-guarantee disclosures close to performance information.
- Never present share-value appreciation as total investor return when cash
  dividends are excluded.
- Do not imply that Sharia compliance, asset security, or investment returns
  have been independently certified unless supporting evidence is available.
- Keep contact details and funding-round information current.
- Make Arabic the primary language and preserve correct RTL reading order.

## Scope boundaries

The current page is an informational landing page, not an investor account
portal. It does not currently provide sign-up, authentication, transaction
execution, portfolio management, or a backend data feed. Actual share values
are shown in the page and recorded in `valuation.csv`. Cash-dividend payment
records have not been supplied; the displayed per-share distributions and
total return are therefore derived from the 50/50 allocation rule rather than
reconciled payment transactions. Projections and the return calculator should
remain unpublished until assumptions are revalidated against the current
policy.

## Success signals

- Visitors can distinguish share-value change from total investor return and
  cash dividends.
- Visitors can review actual valuation dates and values on desktop and mobile.
- Visitors can find and use the contact actions.
- The page remains readable and usable in Arabic RTL layouts across screen
  sizes.

## Open product questions

- Which source and approval process governs actual share valuations, and how
  often will `valuation.csv` be updated?
- Can the derived quarterly dividend amounts be reconciled against payment
  records, including payment dates and any investor-level variation?
- Which validated assumptions should be used to rebuild projections under the
  50/50 profit-allocation policy?
- What evidence and disclosures should accompany claims about Sharia
  compliance, asset coverage, and risk?
- What current funding-round information, if any, should be published?
